## Contradictions and restraint

Museum opening reports give 1,791 and approximately 2,000 objects. Neither total is needed or published in these additions. Marina reporting carries a later update date than its URL; the opening year comes from its operator. Marsa Alam's governorate page has floating relative chronology; no date was calculated from it. Giftun's modern conservation history is not presented as an ancient monument. Ras El Bar's historical shelter account is explicitly attributed to Watani; a purported permanent barrier between river and sea is not repeated.

## Outcome

History coverage rises from 141 to 146 of the existing 154 places. Eight entries still need stronger evidence; no filler history is invented to close that count. Existing sections and all photo records are preserved. Build, unit tests and mobile visual tests cover the additions in both languages.
