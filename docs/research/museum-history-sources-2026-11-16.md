Its architect, political-event and ancient-origin claims were not added; accounts differ and the narrower Bibliotheca description is sufficient.

- A guessed Ministry Luxor Museum page did not resolve. The discovered Ministry news page and two SIS records were checked instead. The [news page](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/en/news/luxor-museum-is-a-spectacular-museum-that-includes-a-variety-of-rare-artifacts-discovered-in-luxor) supports the broad collection context but is not used to infer current display inventory.
- Older museum policy, collection-count and opening-time details on the SIS pages are not presented as current information.
- Hatshepsut attribution remains explicitly uncertain in Arabic and English; the Cavafy salon and Nubian house remain explicitly reconstructions.
