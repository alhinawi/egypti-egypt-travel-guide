# Sourced craft-shopping leads, 2026-11-16

Twelve existing place pages gain optional buying ideas. This is selective editorial work, not a plan to claim that all 154 attractions contain shops. No new venue, exact shop location, opening schedule, stock, price, booking, product certification or authenticity guarantee is introduced. All existing article sections, nearby corrections and hotel-area labels are preserved.

## Page scope

- Friday Market: market-specific craft and food ideas, with current venue/schedule check.
- Saint Catherine's Monastery: regional embroidery lead, explicitly not an inside-monastery shop.
- El Mahalla: textile-industry product categories with producer-source caveat, no factory/store access promise.
- Faiyum Oasis: Tunis Village ceramics as a separate regional visit.
- Philae, Elephantine, Nubian Museum, Unfinished Obelisk: Aswan-area craft ideas, not on-site merchandise.
- Menna TT69, Hatshepsut: separate West Bank craft shopping, not included in admission.
- Karnak, Luxor Museum: a separate Luxor-city market visit, not on-site goods.

## Not used

The 2020 AFP/Ahram El-Arish embroidery report was read but not turned into current shopping or travel advice. Its pandemic products, distribution details, prices and security context are historical. Generic Aswan commercial guides were inspected as leads, but UNESCO provided a firmer city-craft basis; unsupported shop/quality claims were not used.

## Limits

The source pages establish craft categories and cultural context. They do not certify any vendor, price, product or journey. No new photographs are added because exact craft-product photos with verified reuse rights were not established; the existing real place galleries and photos remain unchanged.
