# Choose a virtual story companion

Requested change: replace four cartoon guide avatars with a larger choice of realistic portraits and working selection.

Twelve fictional adult characters now have distinct generated photoreal portraits, including women wearing hijab and varied ages/appearances. These are not real people, licensed guides, customer testimonials or bookable profiles. The page identifies the characters and portraits as fictional browsing companions, without AI terminology. No credentials, biographies, availability, prices or contact details are invented.

Each selection opens three actual, existing place articles matching the character's editorial theme. These suggestions do not imply a walking route, opening status or booking. Selection is browser-local, persists across reload and language changes, and is never sent to an account or person. Clear selection and blocked-storage feedback are available. The existing admin hide/show control for fictional guide data remains in force, and its exported data now carries all twelve records. Real in-person guide advice stays separate.

Portrait provenance: created for this feature with image generation from a prompt describing fictional adults. A 3-column/4-row sheet was checked visually and cropped into 12 optimized WebP files. No external faces were scraped, no celebrity likeness requested, no new paid service or purchase used.

Validation: production build, both typechecks, 73 unit/API/contrast tests, browser cases for Arabic/English choice and reload, language parity, all twelve recommendation sets, blocked storage and admin-hidden data. Mobile screenshots inspected. Privacy copy names the browser-local choice.
