## Accommodation photos

30 distinct Wikimedia Commons hotel photographs are stored locally in
`public/photos/hotels/`. Exact Commons file pages, authors, licence names and
licence URLs are recorded per photo in `src/lib/hotels.json` and shown on each
hotel card. Images were resized and converted to WebP. The same hotel's image
may appear on several relevant attraction pages; it is never used as an
attraction photo. Photos are archival, not a current-condition guarantee.
